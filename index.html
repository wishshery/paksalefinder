The Method

No phantom discounts

Sale season here is full of inflated "original" prices. Here is what we do about it.

01

We read the source

Every listing is pulled from the brand's own storefront — elan.pk, sanasafinaz.com, khaadi.com — not a reseller, a screenshot, or an affiliate feed.

02

We compare against history

A markdown only counts if the original price actually held before the sale. If a "was" price appeared the same week as the discount, the item is dropped.

03

We refresh every 24 hours

Stock and price move fast in lawn season. Sold-out pieces stay visible but clearly marked, and houses with nothing on sale are listed as such rather than hidden.
